# 20 m land-use model with year-dated OSM labels

Auto-generated by `scripts/lu20/05_eval.py`. 53 physical features, 20 m cells, LightGBM with the repo's settings. Training labels for year *y* use OSM as mapped on 31 December *y* (Geofabrik dated snapshots india-180101, india-210101, southern-zone-240101); before this, every model used OSM downloaded in September 2026.

Models: `year_<y>` is trained on year *y* only; `pooled` on all years together (one model for every year); `osm2026_2023` is the control, 2023 inputs with the old undated labels.

## Caveats

- The land-cover parts of the labels (Dynamic World 2023, WorldCover 2021, JRC to 2021, GHS-BUILT-C 2018) are the same for every year; only the OSM part and the water rule are dated.

- OSM history also reflects mapping effort: fewer polygons in 2017 means fewer labels, not less land use.

- The hand labels are for 2023 only, so 2017 and 2020 are checked by OSM-label CV and map stability only.

- Hand labels are scored at the cell containing each 10 m test point, not relabelled at 20 m.
