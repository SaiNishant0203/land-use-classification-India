## Caveats

- WorldPop is a model, not a census: it spreads 2011-census counts with covariates that include night lights and built-up extent, which our model also uses. It has no residential / non-residential information, so agreement with the split is not inherited from it.

- WorldPop counts where people live. Mixed-use buildings (shops below, homes above) are often mapped non-residential and do house people, and the map's non-residential precision is about 0.35, so b is not expected to be zero.

- WorldPop ends in 2020, so the 2023 map is not checked here.

- Zone totals only, not pixel accuracy; the hand-labelled sample stays the accuracy statement.
